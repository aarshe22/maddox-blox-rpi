Maddox Blox

A brick-toy sandbox. Gather loose bricks, build whatever you like, and repair the broken rainbow bridge.

  • WASD / arrows move · Space jump · F shoot the equipped weapon · E enter/exit house doors
  • Drag to orbit the camera · scroll / pinch to zoom
  • Click / tap to place a brick · X or remove-slot tap to break bricks
  • 1-6 pick a brick type
  • Houses have interiors! Kitchen, bathroom, bedroom, wall TV — go knock.
  • ⚙️ Options: Tiny → Huge maps (bigger playable world: towns, farms, roads, zones), change object counts, regenerate.
  • 🗿 Cross the river to find the giant face-mosaic monument — one 1×1 brick per pixel of the welcome portrait (256×225 = 57,600 bricks).
